**Performance: per-frame wasted render + truncation.** `selector.render(terminal_width)` allocates lines at the full terminal width, but `build_select_overlay` immediately pads/truncates them to `content_width = panel_width - 2*border_width`. This means every frame with a resume/rewind overlay pays for wider formatting, extra ANSI string creation, and then a `truncate_to_width`/`pad_ansi_to_width` pass to throw away the excess. Render the content inside the closure with `content_width` to avoid the wasted work.
