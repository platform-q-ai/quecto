**Performance: avoidable `Vec<String>` clone per frame.** Because the content is rendered before the closure is created, `render_fn` must capture `content_lines` by reference and `clone()` it inside the hot path. The whole title/selector/footer vector and every contained string are copied once per frame. Move the title/footer formatting and `selector.render(content_width)` call inside the closure so it owns the data and returns it without cloning.
